# Customer Stories

Since its launch, AWS Trainium/Inferentia has been rapidly adopted by companies across a wide range of industries and scales. From AI startups to global enterprises, customers who have experienced outstanding price-performance in large-scale language model training and inference workloads are accelerating their transition to AWS-designed chips. In particular, companies such as Anthropic, OpenAI, and ByteDance are achieving business results through large-scale Trainium/Inferentia deployments.

- **Anthropic** — Operates more than one million Trainium chips (~1 GW) to train and deploy Claude models. Trn2-based Claude Sonnet 4.5 delivers 1.4x higher output tokens/s than competitors. [Additional 5 GW partnership signed (April 2026)](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-amazon-compute)
- **OpenAI** — Agreed to secure 2 GW of Trainium for next-generation Frontier and stateful runtime workloads
- **ByteDance** — Cut the cost of analyzing tens of billions of video content items per day by 50% with Inferentia2
- **Amazon Bedrock** — AWS also uses Trainium at scale to run its own services (more than 50% of total token throughput is processed on Trainium)

Explore first-hand testimonials below from customers who have achieved performance improvements and cost optimization with Trainium/Inferentia.
