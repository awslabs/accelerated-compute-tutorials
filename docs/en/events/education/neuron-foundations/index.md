# Neuron Foundations Digest (NFD)

!!! note
    Availability of this training is subject to internal review. If interested, please contact your Account Manager (AM) or Solutions Architect (SA).

> **Format:** Theory session (1–2 hours)<br>
> **Audience:** Decision-makers, technical leaders, and practitioners evaluating AWS AI Chip adoption<br>
> **Level:** Introductory<br>
> **Prerequisites:** Basic understanding of machine learning and accelerators (GPUs, etc.)

## 🎯 Course Overview

A theory-focused course that provides a quick understanding of AWS AI Chip value through Trainium hardware architecture, the Neuron software stack, and real customer case studies.

## 📋 Curriculum

| # | Topic | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Trainium H/W Architecture — NeuronCore-v3, HBM, Instance Families | 30 min |
| 2 | Neuron S/W Stack & Development Ecosystem — vLLM, Native PyTorch, NXDI, NXDT | 40 min |
| 3 | Customer Case Studies — Adoption Background, Results, Lessons Learned | 30 min |
| 4 | Q&A | 20 min |
