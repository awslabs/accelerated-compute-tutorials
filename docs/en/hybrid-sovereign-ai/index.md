# Hybrid & Sovereign AI

**Hybrid AI** unifies fragmented on-prem, edge, and other-cloud compute with AWS — bringing your AI workloads together under one consistent operating model.

**Sovereign AI** adds in-country, in-private-VPC self-hosted models and training for cases where data sovereignty, national policy, or linguistic necessity requires it — including national AI programs and AI Factory initiatives.

## What this section will cover

- Connecting on-prem, edge, and other-cloud compute with AWS
- In-country, in-VPC self-hosted model deployment and training patterns
- Data sovereignty and compliance-driven architectures
- Capacity planning for national AI programs and large-scale initiatives
