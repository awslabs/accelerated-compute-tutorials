# Domain Intelligence

Industry- and domain-specific models, fine-tuned on your data, that can outperform much larger proprietary alternatives for the tasks you actually care about — at dramatically lower cost per value. Domain Intelligence is the "think big" extension of Frugal AI: instead of paying for general-purpose scale you don't use, you build focused models that are smaller, faster, and better for your domain.

## What this section will cover

- Fine-tuning open-weight models for industry- and domain-specific performance
- Data curation and evaluation for domain models
- Serving and optimizing domain models cost-effectively
- When a smaller specialized model beats a larger general one
